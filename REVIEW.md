# Reusable Engineering Audit Brief

**This is a review prompt/template, not a completed audit, order specification override, or release sign-off.** The issue/evidence record and gate decisions are in [REMEDIATION.md](REMEDIATION.md). Draft hardware revision is **1.2.0-dev**, with **1.1.0** the reviewed baseline; determine what is actually implemented from the current sources and reports.

## Scope

Act as a hardware, PCB DFM and procurement reviewer. Read REMEDIATION and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), then the current schematic/PCB, project/rule/library files, reviewed sourcing, build implementation and associated documentation. Include [pcb/ELECTRICAL.md](pcb/ELECTRICAL.md), [pcb/ASSEMBLY.md](pcb/ASSEMBLY.md) and [pcb/DFM_EVIDENCE.md](pcb/DFM_EVIDENCE.md): they distinguish adopted file limits and visually resolved drawings from still-unperformed lot/process acceptance. Missing acceptance is a hold, not permission to assume it exists.

Use [pcb/ENVIRONMENT_EVIDENCE.md](pcb/ENVIRONMENT_EVIDENCE.md) for manufacturer sources and detailed unresolved discrepancies, and [MATERIALS.md](MATERIALS.md) for the latest user-confirmed inventory, reel labels and PSU allocation.

Perform a **read-only audit of design/source files** unless changes are separately requested. Use headless tools and build-owned, non-hidden project-local staging; preserve unrelated work and audit evidence. Reports/generated test outputs do not authorize source edits, commits, uploads, part purchases or fabrication. Record the exact revision/hashes and tool versions reviewed; old successful reports are historical evidence only.

## 1. Electrical And Hub Review

- Verify the exact six-independent-end functional matrix: **RUN Start A/B/C -> T1, End A/B/C -> T2; OFF all six independently isolated; TEST Start A/C -> positive, Start B -> DC negative, End A/B/C independently isolated**. Required ties must be source-side contacts, not permanent field straps. Reject opposite-end B return, both-B negative ties or an End A/C common that masks a cut. Check transfer globally across all switch poles, not just individual break-before-make claims.
- Preserve the WAGO through-splice/PCB-tap architecture. Model actual cable-end connections and local board currents; do not route the full perimeter current through every PCB in the calculation. Confirm transmitter/DC separation, OFF limitations, fence-inactive indication and independent containment in TEST/OFF.
- Reproduce the historical **forward-only** nominal baseline with documented inputs: 41 stations, ideal 36 V, 27.6 ohms per 4 km core, 2.2 kohm/branch, 2.8 V combined forward drop. Expected healthy result **0.8477 A / 30.52 W**, LEDs **15.09 -> 8.04 mA**; five near-source boards **0.1509 A / 5.43 W**; all 41 near-source **1.2375 A / 44.55 W**. The 40 ohm/core, 4.0 V sensitivity result **6.21 mA far-end** is illustrative, not a cable bound or visibility proof.
- Review the [current electrical record](pcb/ELECTRICAL.md) and [DESIGN_BOUNDS](pcb/DESIGN_BOUNDS.md): **35 V feed floor / 40.39597 V upper screen are declared inputs, not enforced limits**; the old 35-37 V proposal was never implemented. The **8.21 ohm/km at 20 degrees C** metal-coated Class 5 ceiling is conditional while the **0.30 mm strand label versus 0.26 mm standard maximum** is reconciled. The new **4.090396 mA nonuniform far-end LED corner at 35 V** includes PR02 tolerances, 5.2 V high-drop screening and 50 uA conditional clamp diversion, not an exhaustive or measured minimum. The loose all-station floor is only **0.195041 mA after diversion**, not predicted brightness. The old 4.6681 mA MBE result is historical. Do not double-count diverted clamp current in source demand or add the disconnected spare's capacity; series-diode reverse leakage and OFF glow remain outside the ideal cut model.
- Test all seven cut-core combinations across the 40 spans, especially the first and 3900-4000 m spans, plus repair/retest sequences, power balance and negative controls for masking connections. Check observability again if protection changes make the rungs bidirectional; the old forward-only model cannot validate a different circuit.
- Review **C4 against its updated scope**, not mandatory survival of installation polarity mistakes. D1-D4 are **Vishay General Semiconductor BYG23T-M3/TR / C145454**. D1/D2 are series; D3/D4 are negative shunts after them, cathode to LED positive/anode to B. Verify actual connections, light/cut behavior and ordinary/transient response. **75 ns reverse recovery is not forward-clamp speed**; typical **9 V / 620 ns forward overshoot at 1.5 A, 12 A/us, 25 C** does not prove <=5 V at the LED. The 1300 V rating and output shunts neither regulate forward pulse current nor protect reversed flying leads. Implementation is not qualification, and the historical active design is not a prerequisite.
- Keep **C5** open until the actual PSU/fuse/GDT system is analyzed and appropriately tested for hard/resistive shorts, ignited and failed GDTs, cable-limited current and powered recovery. Recovery is unresolved, **not an observed latched failure**; conditional arc load lines do not predict extinction/non-extinction. D3/D4 do not alter those upstream paths. Use actual DC interrupting/time-current and hiccup data. Do not infer fuse clearing from `>2 A`, extinction from 36 V being below sparkover, or equal transient sharing from parallel conductors.
- Check transient residual voltage, lead overshoot, RF loading and cable insulation limits. A 470 V DC sparkover rating is not a 470 V transient clamp; component 5/20 kA data does not rate the assembled board, and three parallel earth pins do not establish 72 A capability.
- Bound continuous normal TEST temperatures for the selected **Vishay BCcomponents PR02000202201FA100**, copper-lead **2.2 kohm / 2 W / 1% / +/-250 ppm/K**, not the retired MBE0414 or 1.3 W FeCu PR02. The declared **40.39597 V / zero-drop / PR02 Rmin = 2120.8275 ohm** screen through 125 C gives **19.047268 mA / 0.769432871 W per resistor**, or **1.561875985 A / 63.0935 W for 82 zero-cable branches**. PR02 derates from 2 W at 70 C ambient to zero at 155 C; its specific hot-spot maximum is 220 C. The **122.299 C rating-only ambient ceiling** is not an accepted assembly temperature. Nominal heat remains about 0.50 W each; >=1.00 mm body standoff and typical 75 K/W mounting data are not OneGel thermal results. Qualify the real normal source/solar range and material interfaces; no timer, headline wattage or assumed conductivity closes W3.

## 5. Qualification And Reporting

Separate file verification, supplier acceptance and physical qualification. Review the actual scope/results for workmanship on all five prototypes, normal function, selected negative-voltage protection, continuous potted thermal soak, 5 m minimum-current daylight contrast, full-hub cut behavior, enclosure/material process, RF/site and qualified fault/surge recovery. Do not reinstate the excluded reversed-flying-lead immunity test. Retain an unpotted reference; obtain approval for extra/destructive samples if needed. A DMM-open GDT, dark LED or displayed 0.00 mA is not proof of firing capability, safe reverse voltage or sub-microampere leakage.

Require supplied-reel manufacturing-revision and conditional standard-resistance applicability, plus UV/wet-conduit/impulse evidence, without reopening confirmed cable identity or colours. Require OneGel supplied-lot process clarification and compatibility with PP/TPE, exact Essentra supports/adhesive and WAGO 221-613s; no assumed mixing, self-levelling pour or curing step. Include credible coastal/solar conditions and actual RUN/TEST RF/site qualification. **Inspect and maintain the whole-boundary cattle-fence prohibition instead of requiring energizer-on/off testing in the accepted installation**; changed land use or reintroduced exposure requires reassessment before use. Verify the site-specific earthing/bonding design with regional OEM instructions and a qualified installer; do not prescribe arbitrary unbonded rods, direct fence-core earthing or a default DC-negative bond. Do not infer an assembled IP68/hermetic rating, direct-strike protection or multi-decade life from ENIG, gel or component data.

Report **findings first**, ordered by severity, with file/line or measured feature, reproducible evidence, consequence and a specific closure action. Distinguish a demonstrated defect from absent supplier/test evidence and from an optional improvement. Do not describe every unqualified item as certain hardware failure.

Use **PASS, FAIL, HOLD, NOT RUN** only for a named check and inspected revision, not a blanket executive sign-off. Summarize gates A/B/C separately with remaining conditions and owners. State exactly which commands ran, their results and limits, external sources/dates, untested cases and missing evidence. A successful build means verified manufacturing data for that revision; it does not close C4/C5 or grant field/surge approval. Make no claim that files are ready for upload without inspecting the actual gated release and required manufacturer approvals.
