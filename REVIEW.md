# Reusable Engineering Audit Brief

**This is a review prompt/template, not a completed audit, order specification override, or release sign-off.** The issue/evidence record and gate decisions are in [REMEDIATION.md](REMEDIATION.md). Draft hardware revision is **1.2.0-dev**, with **1.1.0** the reviewed baseline; determine what is actually implemented from the current sources and reports.

## Scope

Act as a hardware, PCB DFM and procurement reviewer. Read REMEDIATION and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), then the current schematic/PCB, project/rule/library files, reviewed sourcing, build implementation and associated documentation. Include [pcb/ELECTRICAL.md](pcb/ELECTRICAL.md), [pcb/ASSEMBLY.md](pcb/ASSEMBLY.md) and [pcb/DFM_EVIDENCE.md](pcb/DFM_EVIDENCE.md): they distinguish adopted file limits and visually resolved drawings from still-unperformed lot/process acceptance. Missing acceptance is a hold for its relevant order/qualification scope, not permission to assume it exists.

For a **board/prototype-only audit**, prioritize P3/P4 and **Gate P file-verified prototype artifacts**, separate from the original A/B/C production/field holds. Preserve switch/environmental qualification without restarting that research; enclosure dry-fit follows prototype receipt, not file generation. Supplier CAM/placement/allocated parts and GDT fit/forming/panel/process acceptance remain explicit order tasks. No upload or order is authorized.

Use [pcb/ENVIRONMENT_EVIDENCE.md](pcb/ENVIRONMENT_EVIDENCE.md) for manufacturer sources and detailed unresolved discrepancies, and [MATERIALS.md](MATERIALS.md) for the latest user-confirmed inventory, reel labels and PSU allocation.

Perform a **read-only audit of design/source files** unless changes are separately requested. Use headless tools and build-owned, non-hidden project-local staging; preserve unrelated work and audit evidence. Reports/generated test outputs do not authorize source edits, commits, uploads, part purchases or fabrication. Record the exact revision/hashes and tool versions reviewed; old successful reports are historical evidence only.

## 1. Electrical And Hub Review

- Verify every RUN/OFF/TEST state for all six independent ends against [INSTALL's functional matrix](INSTALL.md#functional-matrix). **TEST Start A/C -> positive, Start B -> DC negative, End A/B/C independently isolated**; OFF isolates all six individually. Required ties must be source-side contacts, not permanent field straps. Reject opposite-end B return, both-B negative ties or an End A/C common that masks a cut. Check transfer globally across all switch poles, not just individual break-before-make claims.
- Preserve the WAGO through-splice/PCB-tap architecture. Model actual cable-end connections and local board currents; do not route the full perimeter current through every PCB in the calculation. Confirm transmitter/DC separation, OFF limitations, fence-inactive indication and independent containment in TEST/OFF.
- Reproduce the four **historical ideal-36 V, forward-only comparisons** in [ELECTRICAL's calculated results](pcb/ELECTRICAL.md#2-calculated-results): distributed 41-board nominal, five zero-cable-resistance boards, all 41 zero-cable-resistance boards and the higher-resistance/higher-drop sensitivity. Record inputs, branch/source currents and power against the named cases; these are not current cable bounds, measurements or visibility proof.
- Review the [declared source/cable/resistance envelope](pcb/DESIGN_BOUNDS.md#evidence-and-inputs): source screens are not enforced limits, and the historical restricted-voltage proposal was never implemented. Confirm the conditional standard-resistance basis and resolve the labelled-strand/standard construction discrepancy. Use the [TE-prototype nonuniform current cases and loose comparison floor](pcb/DESIGN_BOUNDS.md#normal-current), not retired PS12/PR02/MBE evidence or unreviewed Yageo extrapolations. High-drop and clamp-diversion assumptions are not exhaustive or measured minima or predicted brightness. Do not double-count diverted clamp current in source demand or add the disconnected spare's capacity; series-diode reverse leakage and OFF glow remain outside the ideal cut model.
- Test all seven cut-core combinations across the 40 spans, especially the first and 3900-4000 m spans, plus repair/retest sequences, power balance and negative controls for masking connections. Check observability again if protection changes make the rungs bidirectional; the old forward-only model cannot validate a different circuit.
- Review **C4 against its updated scope**, not mandatory survival of installation polarity mistakes. Verify D1-D4 BYG23T identities against the [BOM](pcb/BOM.csv). D1/D2 are series; D3/D4 are negative shunts after them, cathode to LED positive/anode to B. Verify actual connections, light/cut behavior and ordinary/transient response. **75 ns reverse recovery is not forward-clamp speed**; the [typical forward-recovery evidence](pcb/DESIGN_BOUNDS.md#fault-screen) does not prove <=5 V at the LED. The diode reverse rating and output shunts neither regulate forward pulse current nor protect reversed flying leads. Implementation is not qualification, and the historical active design is not a prerequisite.
- Keep **C5** open until the actual PSU/fuse/GDT system is analyzed and appropriately tested for hard/resistive shorts, ignited and failed GDTs, cable-limited current and powered recovery. Recovery is unresolved, **not an observed latched failure**; conditional arc load lines do not predict extinction/non-extinction. D3/D4 do not alter those upstream paths. Use actual DC interrupting/time-current and hiccup data. Do not infer fuse clearing from `>2 A`, extinction from 36 V being below sparkover, or equal transient sharing from parallel conductors.
- Check transient residual voltage, lead overshoot, RF loading and cable insulation limits. A 470 V DC sparkover rating is not a 470 V transient clamp; component 5/20 kA data does not rate the assembled board, and three parallel earth pins do not establish 72 A capability.
- Verify the [TE evidence correction](pcb/DFM_EVIDENCE.md#te-3521-prototype-selection) against **9-1773463-5, Rev G, 02/2025**, not the unsupported 03/2023 attribution for which no archived copy was found. Check that [model fields](pcb/DESIGN_BOUNDS.md#evidence-and-inputs) distinguish the supported manufacturer TCR magnitude from the assumed resistance reference and linear temperature screen. Do not transfer PS12 TCR test endpoints to TE or treat conditional power screens as measured temperatures.
- Bound continuous TEST temperatures using [thermal screens](pcb/DESIGN_BOUNDS.md#thermal-screens) and [actual mounting evidence](pcb/DFM_EVIDENCE.md#te-rating-and-mounting-basis). TE's four-layer reference differs from this two-layer PCB; catalogue power/derating transfer is conditional, not proof of two-layer failure. Verify actual chip/termination/pad/PCB and material-interface temperatures; PR02 hot-spot, mounted thermal-resistance and axial-standoff data do not apply. SMT changes heat flow, not nominal loss or proven OneGel temperature. W3 remains open, not a Gate P prerequisite; no timer, headline wattage or assumed conductivity closes it.
- Keep TE family voltage ceilings distinct from the power-limited catalogue working-voltage calculation under the [rating/mounting evidence](pcb/DFM_EVIDENCE.md#te-rating-and-mounting-basis). The cited TE sheet does not establish a `2.5 * RCWV` / 5 s overload test or the historical PS one-pulse curves. Require exact-part pulse evidence instead of renaming PS12 test conditions as TE data. No component ceiling is a system voltage or surge rating.

- Verify D1-D4's [SMA land/mask/paste geometry](pcb/ASSEMBLY.md#indicator-sma-and-resistor-assembly) and [placement, pad nets and routing](pcb/ASSEMBLY.md#placement-and-polarity). - Verify the adopted [component hole/pad and pin-envelope schedule](pcb/ASSEMBLY.md#hole-fit-evidence) against source/native drills. - Verify all [maximum-body boxes and courtyards](pcb/ASSEMBLY.md#body-and-courtyard-evidence) against ASSEMBLY/DFM_EVIDENCE: original figures have been visually read and Kefa uses explicit E boxes, not unbounded nominal bodies. ## 5. Qualification And Reporting

Separate file verification, supplier acceptance and physical qualification. Review the actual scope/results under [ACCEPTANCE](ACCEPTANCE.md) for workmanship on all five prototypes, normal function, selected negative-voltage protection, continuous potted thermal soak, 5 m minimum-current daylight contrast, full-hub cut behavior, enclosure/material process, RF/site and qualified fault/surge recovery. Check predeclared limits, uncertainty, per-station records and separation of model results from full-fixture evidence. Do not reinstate the excluded reversed-flying-lead immunity test or call accepted installation damage an immunity PASS. Retain an unpotted reference; obtain approval for extra/destructive samples if needed. A DMM-open GDT, dark LED or displayed 0.00 mA is not proof of firing capability, safe reverse voltage or sub-microampere leakage.

Require supplied-reel manufacturing-revision and conditional standard-resistance applicability, plus UV/wet-conduit/impulse evidence, without reopening confirmed cable identity or colours. Require OneGel supplied-lot process clarification and compatibility with PP/TPE, exact Essentra supports/adhesive and WAGO 221-613s; no assumed mixing, self-levelling pour or curing step. Include credible coastal/solar conditions and actual RUN/TEST RF/site qualification. **Verify recorded inspection/compliance and ongoing maintenance of the whole-boundary cattle-fence prohibition, not energizer-on/off testing of the prohibited exposure**; changed land use or reintroduced exposure requires recorded reassessment before use. Verify the site-specific earthing/bonding design with regional OEM instructions and a qualified installer; do not prescribe arbitrary unbonded rods, direct fence-core earthing or a default DC-negative bond. Do not infer an assembled IP68/hermetic rating, direct-strike protection or multi-decade life from ENIG, gel or component data.

Report **findings first**, ordered by severity, with file/line or measured feature, reproducible evidence, consequence and a specific closure action. Distinguish a demonstrated defect from absent supplier/test evidence and from an optional improvement. Do not describe every unqualified item as certain hardware failure.

Use **PASS, FAIL, HOLD, NOT RUN** only for a named check and inspected revision, not a blanket executive sign-off. Summarize **P separately from the original A/B/C production/field gates**, with remaining conditions and owners. State exactly which commands ran, results and limits, sources/dates, untested cases and missing evidence. A prototype build verifies files with holds deferred; it does not close C4/C5/W3/W1/W4 or grant upload/order/field/surge approval. Do not turn a production-refusal workflow PASS into a publication claim or infer hardware qualification from artifacts.
